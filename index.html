Our mission

Better public spaces
start with participation.

The initiative is intentionally simple: organize responsible cleanups, make participation accessible, and document what was done.

We want to turn local concern into practical action — without pretending to be a government service, without collecting money, and without claiming results that have not happened.

01

Local impact

Focus on public areas in and around Zestafoni where an organized community cleanup can make a practical difference.

02

Clear boundaries

Volunteers should know where an event starts and ends, what they are expected to do, and what they must never touch.

03

Honest documentation

Future events can be documented through dates, general locations, participation information, photographs, and waste-handling outcomes.

Safety

Safety comes before
the cleanup.

Cleanup volunteers should only handle ordinary waste that can be safely collected with the equipment and instructions provided.

If something looks hazardous, do not touch it.

Step away, keep other participants away from it, and immediately inform the event coordinator. Do not attempt to identify, move, open, dismantle or dispose of a suspicious object yourself.

!

Never touch these

  • Needles
  • Medical waste
  • Chemicals
  • Unknown liquids or substances
  • Weapons
  • Explosives or suspicious objects
  • Dead animals
  • Anything else that appears hazardous
✓

Basic participant rules

  • Use gloves and designated cleanup tools.
  • Never enter traffic or an unsafe roadway area.
  • Do not climb buildings, structures, trees or unstable objects.
  • Stay within the announced cleanup area.
  • Report injuries, hazards and unsafe conditions immediately.
  • Stop participating if you feel unwell.
  • Follow the coordinator's instructions throughout the event.
Coordinator-led events

Official events are intended to have a coordinator responsible for the briefing, boundaries and reporting process. Participants should never feel pressured to handle something they consider unsafe.

Waste handling

A cleanup does not
end with a full bag.

Future events should have an appropriate plan for the collection, transfer and disposal of the waste gathered during the activity.

01

Collect responsibly

Waste should be gathered using suitable bags, gloves and designated tools rather than left scattered around the cleanup area.

02

Arrange collection

Before an official event, the organizers intend to clarify appropriate waste collection and disposal arrangements.

03

Document the outcome

Future cleanup records should state what happened to collected waste instead of implying that filled bags were simply abandoned somewhere.

Questions, answered clearly

What you should know
before participating.

These answers describe the project's current position and will be updated when actual event arrangements are established.

Is participation paid?

No. Participation in Zestafoni Forward cleanup activities is voluntary and unpaid.

Do you accept donations?

No. Zestafoni Forward is not currently a charity or fundraising project and does not accept donations or collect money.

How do I know an event is official?

An official Zestafoni Forward cleanup will be announced through the project's official website or official project channels. An activity organized independently by other people is not automatically an official Zestafoni Forward event.

What if I find dangerous waste?

Do not touch it. This includes needles, medical waste, chemicals, unknown liquids or substances, weapons, explosives or suspicious objects, dead animals, and anything else that appears hazardous. Move away and inform the coordinator immediately.

Is Zestafoni Forward a municipal service?

No. Zestafoni Forward is an independent community initiative. It does not claim municipal authority, government status or government endorsement.

Can I organize an event using the Zestafoni Forward name?

An independent activity should not be presented as an official Zestafoni Forward event unless it has been authorized and announced through the project's official channels.

Will there be official cleanups soon?

No date has been announced yet. The first public event will only be listed once the organizers have established the necessary operating, safety and waste-handling arrangements.

Does the project work with the municipality?

No municipal cooperation is currently being claimed. The organizers intend to speak with the relevant local authorities before the first public cleanup to clarify practical arrangements and possible future cooperation.
