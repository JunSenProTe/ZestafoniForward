Questions, answered honestly

What you should know
before getting involved.

The project is still being established. These answers will be updated as concrete information becomes available.

Why can't I donate yet?

The organization is not legally established yet, and the donation system is not operational. We do not want to accept money before the appropriate legal, financial, and accountability systems are ready.

When will donations become available?

There is no confirmed launch date yet. The website will be updated when legal setup, hospital relationships, and the transparent donation process are ready.

Where will donations go?

The intended focus is local hospitals and the surrounding healthcare community in Zestafoni. Future uses will be based on legitimate needs confirmed by the relevant hospitals.

How will you prove where money went?

The project intends to publish campaign goals, amounts raised, expenses, appropriate documentation, and confirmation of results. The exact reporting system will be finalized before donations begin.

Can I suggest something a hospital needs?

Yes, suggestions will be welcomed once the community-request system is ready. A suggestion will not automatically mean a need is confirmed or a campaign is approved.

Which hospitals will you support?

No hospital partnership or support list is being claimed at this stage. The project will publish confirmed relationships when they exist.

Who is behind the project?

This is a local initiative currently being developed. Founder, team, and organizational information will be published when it is finalized and appropriate to share.

Is the organization legally registered yet?

No. The legal setup is still in progress. This website does not claim registered-charity status, tax-deductible donations, or current fundraising activity.
